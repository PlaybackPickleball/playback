- Smart Court Fill (job 7, server only, no UI): `server/src/play/fillSchema.ts`, `server/src/play/smartFill.ts`, routes on `routes.ts`. Tables `promotion_rules`, `fill_publications`, `fill_requests`, `revenue_attributions` (CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS). A facility sets its own normal price, promo price, max discount percent and promotable hours, and Smart Fill stays off until that facility enables it. Nothing reprices a slot on its own, and the listed slot price is never rewritten. Publishing (`POST /api/play/admin/smart-fill/publish`) is refused when the feature is off, the slot hour is outside the window, or the discount from either the owner's normal price or the slot's listed price exceeds `max_discount_percent`. A publish that passes calls `createOpenMatch` at the promo price (one held booking, per-player price from that court price). Players ask to join; the facility approves or rejects. Approval seats the player through `joinOpenMatch` and does not create a second booking. Rejection adds no participant. A direct join on a published game is refused until that request is approved. Joining does not write ratings. `revenue_attributions` stores `booking_id`, `origin` (`playback` or `facility`), `amount_vnd`, and `incremental` defaulting to 0. Ordinary Playback bookings, demo open matches and queue bookings stay non-incremental. Only the booking created by a Smart Fill publish is flagged incremental. `GET /api/play/admin/smart-fill/summary` sums those stored rows: gross booking value, Playback-originated total, and estimated incremental only where the flag is true. Other admin routes: get/put settings, list vacant demo slots, disable. Demo auth is unchanged (`x-player-id`, `x-admin-token`). Demo seed inserts one disabled rule per demo facility (`demo = 1`, 150,000 / 120,000 VND, 20% cap, 17:00–21:00), INSERT OR IGNORE, and demo reset restores those demo rules without deleting bookings or touching a `demo = 0` rule. `resetDemo` also keeps players referenced by `fill_requests`. Tests: `smartFill.test.ts` (6). Pre-change copy: `data/court-replay-pre-fill-20261016-061533.db` (gitignored). `web/src` was not edited.

- Smart Queue (job 6, server only, no UI): `server/src/play/queueSchema.ts`, `queuePolicy.ts`, `queue.ts`, routes in `routes.ts`. Tables `queue_settings` (mode `fifo` | `skill` | `hybrid`, default `hybrid`), `queue_entries`, `queue_offers`, `queue_audit`. One active `waiting` / `offered` / `confirmed` entry per player (partial unique index). Join from the app, a court QR (`court_qr` needs a court), or staff (`POST /api/play/admin/queue/join` with `x-admin-token`). Cancel and offer expiry release the court; a waiting entry that is not a demo row expires after `PLAY_QUEUE_ENTRY_TTL_MINUTES` (default 180). Assignment is pure (`selectGroup`): fifo by join time; skill picks the closest ratings inside the band (default 0.5 levels) or offers nothing; hybrid starts from join order and may skip ahead only when the match is at least `minSkillGain` (0.3) tighter, inside the band, and nobody would then have been passed by more than `maxBypass` (3) later players, counting earlier rounds. `POST /api/play/admin/queue/court-ready` creates one offer with an acceptance window (default 120s, the slot becomes `queue_managed`). Accept runs through `applyTransition` in `bookings.ts` and creates exactly one confirmed booking once the whole group has accepted. Decline or timeout returns the others to the queue and offers the court to the next group; `sweepQueue` expires overdue offers. Public status (`GET /api/play/queue/status`) shows display name and rating level only, plus place, party size and state, with waiting count, position and nearer facilities that have a shorter queue. Estimated wait stays `not_available_yet` until five real (non-demo) assignments, and even then it is a range with `waitMinutes: null`, never an exact promise. Play Now `join_queue` uses that same length and estimate. Demo auth is unchanged: `x-player-id` and `x-admin-token`. Demo queue seed is six waiting players at `demo-facility`, deterministic ids per venue day, `demo = 1`, insert-only. Schema was already additive (`CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`); this pass did not change it and did not take a new database backup.
  - Routes: `POST /api/play/queue/join`, `POST /api/play/queue/:id/cancel`, `GET /api/play/queue/status`, `GET /api/play/queue/my`, `POST /api/play/queue/offers/:id/accept`, `POST /api/play/queue/offers/:id/decline`. Admin: `GET /api/play/admin/queue`, `GET|PUT /api/play/admin/queue/settings`, `POST /api/play/admin/queue/join`, `POST /api/play/admin/queue/:id/cancel`, `POST /api/play/admin/queue/court-ready`, `GET /api/play/admin/queue/audit`.
  - Tests: `queuePolicy.test.ts` (14, pure fifo / skill / hybrid limit) and `queue.test.ts` (10: uniqueness, cancel, fifo, skill, hybrid limit, decline and timeout, concurrent accept creates one booking, audit, privacy and the wait range, idempotent seed). `openMatches.test.ts` now expects the demo queue length of 6 at the main club and still `not_available_yet`.
